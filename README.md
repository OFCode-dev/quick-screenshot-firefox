# Quick Screenshot → Clipboard (Firefox)

Copy the **visible area** of your current tab to your clipboard as a **PNG screenshot** — with **one click**.  
No editing. No downloads. Just click → paste.

## Why it’s useful
If you work across devices (MacBook ↔ iPad, notes ↔ chats ↔ docs), screenshots become a constant “micro-task”.  
This extension removes the friction: **one click copies**, then you **paste anywhere**.

## How it works
1. Open any webpage
2. Click the extension icon
3. Paste with **Cmd+V** (or **Ctrl+V**) into Notes, ChatGPT, Slack, Docs, email, tickets, etc.

## Example workflow (my favorite)
- Click the icon to copy a screenshot
- Paste it into ChatGPT
- Get your answer / fix / solution from the screenshot — fast

## Privacy
- **No accounts**
- **No tracking**
- **No analytics**
- **No data collection**
- Everything runs **locally** in your browser

## Limitations
Browser security may block screenshots on internal/protected pages such as:
- `about:`
- `moz-extension:`
- `addons.mozilla.org`
…and some other restricted tabs.
